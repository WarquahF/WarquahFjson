Most tools I use daily would survive their company disappearing tomorrow
with barely a change: a compiler, an editor, a terminal. Most web apps
would not. That asymmetry is the whole argument.

Local-first, for me, comes down to three properties:

- The software runs on the machine in front of you.
- The data lives where you can see it and back it up yourself.
- The network is an enhancement, not a requirement.

It's harder to build this way. It's also easier to trust.
